Quince Demo: What It's Testing

Not a status log — the thesis the build exists to prove, and what it would take to see it land.

The thesis

Grounded retrieval plus honest escalation, mirroring Kinect's own line: "built into your store, not bolted on." The rep answers from a real 42-SKU catalog, escalates account and order questions rather than guessing, and admits gaps instead of hallucinating.

  • Catalog search resolves real pre-purchase questions (e.g. "cashmere sweater under $100")
  • Policy questions answered from verified facts, not invented
  • Account/order questions escalate — Kinect's own product deliberately does the same
  • Out-of-catalog asks are admitted, not hallucinated
Behavior above is grounding-tested and passing. End-to-end UI send/reply/close is not yet confirmed.
